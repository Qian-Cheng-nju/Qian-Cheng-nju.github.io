I am Qian Cheng, a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at Nanjing University. My research lies at the intersection of formal methods, software systems, and artificial intelligence. I develop agentic tools for formal specification, model checking, and formal proof of complex real-world systems.

## Research Interests

My primary research area is **formal methods for real-world systems**, especially model checking and specification-driven assurance. I am also exploring how **generative AI** can help create and refine formal specifications for complex computer systems. I am particularly interested in:
- AI-assisted authoring and maintenance of formal specifications
- Generative AI techniques that bridge software engineering and formal methods

## Projects

- [Specular](https://github.com/specular-org/Specular) (formerly Specula): An agentic system that generates and validates TLA+ specifications, uses model checking to find deep bugs, and reproduces them in real system code. [[Website](https://specularhq.ai/)] [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25333)]
- [TLAPS-Bench](https://github.com/specular-org/TLAPS-Bench): A benchmark for evaluating AI's ability to write machine-checked TLAPS proofs, covering both proof completion and proof construction from scratch. [[Website](https://specular-org.github.io/tlaps-bench-website/)] [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02849)]
- [SysMoBench](https://github.com/specular-org/SysMoBench): Benchmark suite and artifacts for system modeling and runtime verification. [[Website](https://sysmobench.com/)]
